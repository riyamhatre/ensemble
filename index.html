    What's on

    Weekly Broadway grosses from June 1985 to March 2023, Tony Award nominations and wins from 1985 to 2026, Broadway cast albums from MusicBrainz, and New York Times coverage looked up live.

    Who it's for

    Theater fans who want the numbers behind the gossip, and producers and analysts reading the market: what moves a show's box office, and how it compares with the rest of Broadway the same weeks.

    How to ask

    • Name the show, and the years if it matters.
    • Ask about change: after a Tony, a review, the reopening.
    • Open Backstage to watch every agent's context as it works.

    What it can't tell you

    Box office after March 2023, profit or recoupment, audience demographics, nightly numbers, tours and off-Broadway. The report says so when a question reaches any of these.

    Grosses originally from Playbill, via the Tidy Tuesday and IST719 datasets. Tony Awards from Wikipedia. Cast albums from MusicBrainz (CC0). Press data provided by The New York Times.
